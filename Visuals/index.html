Interactive map

Municipality map

Year and week selectors, age-standardised rate and temperature switch. Runs entirely in your browser.

Rate = n / pop, age-standardised to the Austrian age distribution of the same year and shown as a raw proportion (about 0.0000–0.0123). Age 1 is excluded (no population denominator).

Vienna is shown by its 23 districts. Colour scales are fixed across weeks; the rate scale is capped at the 99th percentile.
