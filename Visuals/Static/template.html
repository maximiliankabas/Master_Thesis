Weekly hospitalisations and temperature in Austria

Municipality-level interactive map, 2009–2018

Indicator

Blue = low values, red = high values. Rates are age-standardised to the Austrian age distribution of the same year (direct standardisation, ages 0–95), so districts with different age structures are comparable. The colour scale is capped at the 99th percentile so that very small municipalities with extreme rates do not dominate the map.

Grey areas have no data for the selected indicator. Vienna is shown by its 23 districts.
